# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Sigillum a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, especially around wallet and security information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when needed, and learn from mistakes.
- Put the safety of the community and its users ahead of individual preference.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
disclosure of private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[security@caelator.com](mailto:security@caelator.com). Project
maintainers will review reports confidentially and may remove content, restrict
participation, or ban contributors when conduct is harmful.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
